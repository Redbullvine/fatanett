Paytable & rules

10 fixed lines. Matches start on reel 1 and run left to right. Three or more consecutive symbols pay. Only the highest win per line pays; line wins add together.

Numbers multiply the line wager (total wager ÷ 10). Wild substitutes for all paying symbols, never scatter. Three or more Luna Moon SCATTER symbols anywhere trigger one bonus choice, with no separate scatter payout.

Showdown pays 5× to 100× total wager with weighted probabilities. Heidi pays a 5× welcome award plus ten free spins with 4× line wins. Maximum 5,000× across the triggering spin and entire feature. No retriggers.
